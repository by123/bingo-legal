What Bingo does — and what iOS does not allow

Bingo reads a task list, draws it as an image sized to your iPhone's Lock Screen, and hands that image to the Shortcuts app. Two platform limits are part of the product, and you should understand them before relying on it:

  • No app can set your wallpaper. iOS provides no public API for it. The final step is always performed by a shortcut on your device using Apple's built-in "Set Wallpaper" action, which you install and run yourself.
  • The wallpaper is not live. It updates only when one of those shortcuts runs. Between runs, the image on your Lock Screen may not reflect your latest tasks.

Tapping a Lock Screen button also requires unlocking your device first — that is how iOS treats shortcuts that run app actions, and it is not something the App can change.
